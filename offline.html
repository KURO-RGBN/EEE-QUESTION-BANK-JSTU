How To Play 2048

  • 1. Swipe screen or use the arrow cluster pad to shift all the numeric blocks in that direction.
  • 2. When two squares carrying matching values touch each other, they **fuse into one containing their combined sum** (2+2=4, 4+4=8).
  • 3. Every move spawns a new block. Keep fusing blocks to achieve the ultimate goal: the **2048 tile**!
