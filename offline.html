How To Play 2048

  • 1. Swipe left, right, up, or down directly on the dark grid squares to shift all numeric tiles.
  • 2. When two tiles with matching numbers collide, they **merge into one tile containing their sum**!
  • 3. Every slide spawns a new block. Keep combining them to clear space and reach higher tiles.
